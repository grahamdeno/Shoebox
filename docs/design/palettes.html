Shoebox color range

Eight palettes that differ in kind, not only in hue: light or dark, one color or many, quiet or loud, flat or glassy. Every card shows the same two moments, so the only thing changing is color.

Faded Print · light, warmVelvet · dark, richGraphite · no colorTomato · one loud colorSorbet · many soft colorsGlass · gradient and frostedForest · dark, earthyMidnight Gold · dark, classic

Faded Print

LightWarmMuted

Cream paper, plum to act, peach for new, faded cyan for done. Calm, personal, a little nostalgic. The baseline from last round.

Velvet

DarkWarmRich

Plum-black everywhere, coral to act, lilac for done. Feels like evening. The app and the Lock Screen look like one thing.

Graphite

LightNo colorEditorial

Black, white and grey only. All the character comes from type. Your photos become the only color on the screen.

Tomato

SaturatedOne colorLoud

One bold color, used everywhere, with black type and black buttons. Impossible to miss on a Lock Screen. Confident, poster-like.

Sorbet

LightMany colorsPlayful

Each save gets its own soft color: pink, lavender, mint, butter. Deep ink, black outlines with a small offset shadow. Friendly and lively.

Glass

GradientFrostedVery iOS

A warm gradient behind frosted glass cards, the way iPhone's own system looks now. Modern and native, less personal.

Forest

DarkEarthyGrounded

Deep green, bone type and persimmon to act. Outdoorsy and steady, like a field journal.

Midnight Gold

DarkClassicFormal

Navy, cream type and a soft gold. Feels like a well-made leather notebook or an old library.
