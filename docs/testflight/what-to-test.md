Thanks for trying Shoebox. Please try these and tell me what felt wrong.

New here? Watch the one-minute setup video: https://inmyshoebox.com/site/shoebox-getting-started-60.mp4

1. Keep a link from Safari with the share sheet. Add a reason.
2. Keep something with the + button: a note, a photo, a PDF.
3. On Today: flick up for Next, pull down to Put away. Tap to open a post, then Close.
4. Turn on the daily notification in Settings, then see if it arrives.
5. Settings > Email: It needs Shoebox Plus (free in TestFlight). Get your address, forward one real email to it, then reopen Shoebox.

If something breaks, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback (take a screenshot first) or write support@inmyshoebox.com.
