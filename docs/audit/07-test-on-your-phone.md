# Test on your phone this week

Things this audit could not verify: no Swift compiler, FoundationModels can't run in the cloud, no device, no live relay calls.

1. **Update from build 36.** Before installing a 2.0 build over Shoebox 1, save a backup in Shoebox 1. Then check every save, the email address and photos come across. Repeat once without a fresh backup and note what is lost.
2. **Writing surface after PR #28.** Keep something, the share sheet from Safari, and Edit on an opened post: type, change the date, Keep, leave, come back.
3. **Close on the opened post.** Tap a post twice fast, then Close. Open Edit, close it, then tap Close straight away. Open a PDF in Quick Look, close it, then Close. Note which one fails.
4. **Share 10 full-size camera photos** at once from Photos. Does the share sheet close or crash?
5. **20 real forwards:** receipts, a flight, a hotel, a school notice, a quote, an Outlook forward (it adds "Sent:"), a newsletter PDF. For each, note: wrong date, invented detail, missing time, good post kept as plain text.
6. **Apple Intelligence off** (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri): forward three emails and save two links with no picture. Is anything stuck or blank?
7. **Delete the app and reinstall.** Is the email address gone? Does a Shoebox backup bring it back?
8. **New-phone restore.** In Files, can you find Shoebox › Backups in iCloud Drive? Can Settings › Backups › Bring one back open a 2.0 backup?
9. **Largest text size** (Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Larger Text): Today, the opened post, Settings. Note what clips.
10. **VoiceOver on Today:** swipe right through the screen with the Shoebox closed. Do you hear Shoebox rows or the search field?
11. **Reduce Motion on:** flick a post up. Does the box still shake?
12. **A bright photo post** (a white sky or snow): can you read the "+ New", "1 of N" and the byline?
13. **Region set to United Kingdom:** forward an email with "10/11" in it. Which date does it pick?
14. **Stock pictures:** after the relay deploy, keep three plain notes ("Dentist", "Buy a tent", "Soccer practice"). Do pictures appear by the next open, and is none of the dentist one looked up?
