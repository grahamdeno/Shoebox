## Description

Shoebox keeps the things you would otherwise lose: a school notice, a link, a photo of a flyer, a note to yourself. You keep it, add a few words about why, and it comes back to you when it matters.

Keep from anywhere. Use the share sheet in Safari, Mail, Photos or any app, or tap + New to type a note, add a photo or choose a file.

Say why. A few words of your own sit above each post, so you know later why you kept it.

Dates are read for you. Event days, times and deadlines are found in what you keep. A post about Saturday comes back before Saturday, and past events move out of the way on their own.

One post at a time. Today shows a single post, full screen. Flick up for the next. Pull down to put it away. Nothing is ever deleted by accident, and Undo is there for five seconds.

Your Shoebox holds everything not done, and a search finds anything, including what you put away.

Email it in (Shoebox Plus). Forward a newsletter or school email to your own Shoebox address. Shoebox writes a short post from what matters in it, and an email with many dates becomes one post per date about the people you name. Mail is sealed to a key only your phone holds, so Shoebox's server cannot read it. Shoebox Plus is a one-time purchase, offered after your first week.

Private by design. There is no account. Your posts stay on your phone. Reading, dates and pictures are worked out on the phone. Only link previews, generic stock-picture words and forwarded email pass through Shoebox's server, and nothing about you is sold or used to track you.

Also: one daily notification at the time you choose, daily backups to Files and iCloud Drive, and Delete everything in Settings.

Best on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. Without it, Shoebox reads posts with plain rules.

## Review notes

Shoebox has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything stays on the phone except link previews, generic stock-picture words and forwarded email. AI runs on the device; on a device without Apple Intelligence, posts use plain rules. Notifications are local: one a day at a time the user picks.

Demo path:
1. Finish the four welcome screens.
2. Tap + New, paste any link, add a reason, tap Keep.
3. Pull the post down to put it away. Flick up for the next one.
4. To delete: Settings > Delete everything removes the posts and the email address on the server.

**Email address and Shoebox Plus.** The email address needs Shoebox Plus, a one-time non-consumable purchase. In review and TestFlight builds the Plus offer is shown at once: Settings > Email, then "Get your address with Shoebox Plus". (Customers on the App Store see it 7 days after first opening the app.) After buying, enter a first name, tap Get address, forward any email to it and reopen Shoebox. Restore purchases is in Settings.

## Decided: how a reviewer reaches Plus (option 1, November 2)

A reviewer opens a fresh install and will not wait 7 days. Apple expects an in-app purchase submitted with the app to be reachable in review, so hiding it for a week risks a rejection. Options, recommended first:

1. Show the Plus offer at once when the app runs in the App Store sandbox (the reviewer's and TestFlight's environment, `AppStore.environment == .sandbox`). Real customers on the store still see it after 7 days, and TestFlight testers see it right away instead of waiting a week. A small code change, and the review note then says the offer appears at once in review builds. Sonnet builds it, Opus reviews.
2. Give the reviewer a path in the notes, such as a hidden tap. Not recommended: harder to keep honest.
3. Drop the 7-day wait for 1.0. Simplest, but shows the offer in the first run, which SPEC 9 rules out.
