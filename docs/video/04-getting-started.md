# Video 4: getting started

Follow `docs/video/style.md` for everything not said here.

**Job:** help a new beta tester set Shoebox up in a minute. **Where:** linked from the TestFlight "What to Test" note and the support page. **Shape:** 9:16, 45 to 60 seconds, voice and captions. Practical, not a pitch: no "Join the beta" end card. End on "Questions? support@inmyshoebox.com".

| # | Picture | Voice | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `light-welcome` | Welcome to Shoebox. Here's how to set it up. | Setting up Shoebox |
| 2 | `light-share`, spotlight step 1 then 2. | In any app, tap Share, then Shoebox. If it's not in the row, swipe to the end, tap More, and add it. | Share, then Shoebox |
| 3 | `light-share`, spotlight step 3. | Say why you're keeping it. "For the trip" brings it back before the trip. | Say why |
| 4 | `light-add` | You can also keep things right in the app, with plus New. | Or tap + New |
| 5 | `light-time` | Pick when your posts come out each morning, and turn on the one daily notification. | Pick your time |
| 6 | `light-learn` | Then three moves. | Three moves |
| 7 | `light-up`, `light-away`, `light-opened`, one after another with the thumb dot. | Flick up for the next one. Pull down when you're done. Tap to open. | Up. Down. Tap. |
| 8 | `light-box` | Everything waits in your Shoebox. Search finds anything, even what you put away. | Your Shoebox |
| 9 | `light-settings`, spotlight Email. | To forward email, open Settings, then Email, and get your own address. | Settings, then Email |
| 10 | Pistachio card | Questions? Write to support at in my shoebox dot com. | support@inmyshoebox.com |

**Needs a screenshot that doesn't exist yet:** shot 9 would be clearer on the Email screen itself (`light-email`). Until CI captures one, use `light-settings` with the spotlight on the Email row, or leave the spotlight off if Email isn't shown.
