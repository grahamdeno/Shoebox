# Photos for the sample posts

Used only by `Scripts/screens.sh` for simulator screenshots and the promo video. They are copied into the
simulator, never into the app.

| File | For the post | What it should show |
|---|---|---|
| `concert.jpg` | Fall band concert (the post the video leads with) | A school band or a stage with instruments and warm light. No faces you can recognize. |
| `trail.jpg` | A trail worth the drive | A coastal trail or a lookout over the sea. |
| `cookout.jpg` | Cast iron care | A cast iron pan or a grill. |
| `teams.jpg` | Why small teams ship faster | A desk, a whiteboard or a notebook. No logos. |

Rules: each photo must be Graham's own or carry a license that allows commercial use with no attribution
(Pixabay or Unsplash license, or CC0). Write where each came from in `SOURCES.md`. No recognizable faces,
no children, no names, no house numbers or plates, no brand logos. JPEG, about 1600 px wide.
